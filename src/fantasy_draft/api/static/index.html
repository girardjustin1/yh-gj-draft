How this works

  1. Set your draft slot — the button in the next-pick panel.
  2. Record every pick as it happens, including other managers'. Find him under Players, then tap the blue DRAFT button if you took him, or swipe the row left if someone else did. That is how the app knows who is left.
  3. When it is your turn, the Pick tab tells you who to take and what to aim for at the pick after.
